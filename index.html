About the exhibit

How the alignment works

The core is treated as one continuous 0–163 cm profile. CT videos provide segment context; quantitative comparison uses the physical profiles and chemical samples.

  1. Brine, porosity, and density retain their original 1 cm resolution.
  2. Physical values are overlap-weighted into 5 cm chemical intervals.
  3. The final chemical sample is explicitly integrated over 155–163 cm.
  4. Missing observations remain missing and coverage is reported; zeros are never substituted.
